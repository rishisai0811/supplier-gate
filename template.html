What this review does and doesn't prove
This is an exact text comparison, not a store-specific validator or a promise that an import is safe. It does not validate prices, tax rules, stock quantities, product URLs or required platform fields. Reordered rows and columns are matched by header and selected key. Column additions or removals always require review. A changed key is shown as a removal plus addition. Whitespace, leading zeros and numeric formatting are not normalized. Malformed quotes, unequal row widths, empty headers and duplicate headers are rejected rather than guessed. Empty physical lines are not silently discarded.
Only UTF-8 is supported, with or without BOM. Maximum 10 MB per file and 50,000 data rows. Everything runs in memory. Close or reload to erase the session. No storage, analytics, network requests, AI, payments or store access. Downloaded reports stay wherever you save them. Keep an original export and use your platform's preview or staging environment before a live import.
